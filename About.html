Life @ Tata Motors ->

Pursuant to the Composite Scheme of Arrangement approved by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, effective
October 1, 2025, Tata Motors has demerged its Commercial Vehicles business into TML Commercial Vehicles Limited
(TMLCV), a wholly owned subsidiary and consolidated the Passenger Vehicles business within the parent
entity.
Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles business begins a bold new chapter as an independently listed entity. Ranked among India’s top
three car manufacturers and the market leader in the electric vehicle (EV) segment, the company continues to redefine mobility
for the modern Indian consumer
With a legacy of innovation—from India’s first indigenous car to shaping the SUV and EV landscape—the company blends design-forward
thinking with a robust multi-powertrain strategy to meet diverse lifestyle needs. Each vehicle is crafted to deliver an exceptional driving
experience, powered by intuitive technology and segment-leading features.
The company’s deep-rooted commitment to safety and customer delight is reflected across every touchpoint with each Tata Motors
vehicle evoking pride, satisfaction, and a meaningful connection with today’s discerning buyer.

With emphasis on decarbonisation, circular economy and preservation of the natural environment, we are integrating sustainability in our business strategy.
